Privacy Policy

P

Effective Date: 29 September 2026

IndianCoinInfo.site does not ask you to provide personal details or use analytics to track visitors. Some information may still be processed by your browser, our hosting provider, and external resources as described below.

Information You Provide

  • We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses.
  • We do not require users to register or log in.
  • We do not provide forms that request personal details.

Browser Storage

The Site uses your browser's local storage to remember settings such as your selected theme and to keep your coin collection choices on your device. This information is not submitted to us by these features. Clearing your browser's site data may remove it.

If you use the backup feature, your browser downloads a JSON file containing all of this Site's local storage entries. Restoring a backup reads the file on your device and replaces the Site's local storage entries in your browser. These features do not upload the file to us. Keep downloaded backups private if you do not want others to see your saved settings and collection choices.

Cookies and Analytics

We do not currently use analytics services or set cookies for tracking visitors.

Website Operator

This website is operated by the creator of IndianCoinInfo.site, an informational resource focused on Indian numismatics. The website is intended solely for educational and reference purposes.

Server Log Files

Our hosting provider may automatically record requests to the Site, including IP addresses, browser information, referring pages, and timestamps. Such logs are handled by the provider for purposes such as security, diagnostics, and website operation; they are separate from the Site's browser storage.

Third-Party Links

IndianCoinInfo.site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. Please review their privacy policies individually.

External Resources

The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for details of how it handles that information.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
